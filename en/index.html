Pet Hotel & Dog Day Care in Uji, Kyoto

LESSON WON is a small, home-based pet hotel in Uji, Kyoto, run by a professional dog trainer. We take only a few dogs at a time, and someone is at home around the clock, so your dog keeps its usual routine with morning and evening walks.

Services

Overnight boarding

From ¥4,500 per day (small dogs up to 6 kg), ¥5,500 (up to 20 kg), ¥6,000 (up to 30 kg). Larger dogs and cats: please ask. Peak seasons (Golden Week, Obon, New Year) add ¥500 per day.

Hourly day care

From ¥1,000 for the first hour, then ¥500 per 30 minutes. No membership fee. Handy while you visit Byodoin Temple or go out for a few hours.

Training & day school

Dog training and a day school for socialization are also available. These pages are in Japanese: training, day school.

All prices include tax. Cash only.

Before you book

  • All services are by reservation only. Hours 8:00–20:00.
  • Please bring proof of vaccination (rabies and combination vaccine), your dog's usual food, collar and leash.
  • Dogs in heat cannot be accepted. Flea and tick prevention must be up to date.
  • Cancellation: 60% two days before, 80% the day before, 100% on the day.
  • First-time guests start with a short trial stay so your dog can get used to the house.
